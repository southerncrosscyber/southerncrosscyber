About Southern Cross Cyber

Southern Cross Cyber was established in 2019 as a Sydney-based cybersecurity consultancy specialising in detection engineering, threat intelligence and incident response advisory.

The firm worked with enterprise and government organisations across Australia to design measurable detection strategies and improve security operations maturity.

Our approach focused on data-driven engineering outcomes, ensuring that reported security metrics reflected operational reality.

Operations concluded in 2021 following the completion of core engagements and strategic restructuring.
