Company Updates

Operational Transparency & Detection Metrics

March 2021 — D. Kearney

Over the past two years, Southern Cross Cyber has prioritised measurable detection engineering outcomes. Our internal review processes ensure that every reported metric reflects validated operational performance rather than projected capability.

Detection effectiveness must be defensible. Engineering teams are required to document logic development, testing methodologies and validation procedures prior to executive reporting.

Professional debate within engineering teams is expected and encouraged. However, final reporting must reflect reviewed and approved operational data.

Scaling Detection Engineering Across Enterprise Environments

November 2020 — Engineering Team

As threat actors increased operational sophistication throughout 2020, detection pipelines required continuous refinement. Our engineers focused on telemetry enrichment, behavioural correlation and improved signal-to-noise optimisation.

Cross-functional collaboration between intelligence analysts and detection engineers allowed us to maintain adaptive response strategies across client environments.

We acknowledge that rapid growth phases can create alignment challenges within technical teams. Structured review cycles were implemented to ensure consistency across engagements.

Internal Review & Strategic Realignment

July 2020 — Executive Update

Following completion of several major engagements, Southern Cross Cyber initiated an internal review of engineering methodologies and reporting alignment.

As part of this process, certain team roles were restructured to better reflect strategic direction and operational standards.

We remain confident in our technical foundation and leadership oversight during transitional periods.

Building Measurable Security Programs

February 2020 — D. Kearney

Security programs often fail due to over-reliance on theoretical coverage metrics. Our approach has always been grounded in validated detection logic and reproducible results.

Clients require assurance that detection capabilities are engineered, tested and maintained — not assumed.
